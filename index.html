Rotation style (optional)

Keep players in their positions: a player marked PG, SG, SF, PF or C only plays that spot, G plays either guard spot and F either forward spot. A player with several positions can play all their spots (e.g. PG + SG). Players without a position can play anywhere. Players with specific positions are never moved; if the roster cannot fill a position, only plain G / F players may help out the nearest group, and minutes may be adjusted. The app explains any change.

How it works
  • In – untick players who miss this game. They move to the bottom with 0 minutes; tick them back in to restore their original place and previous minutes setting.
  • Min – minutes for the game. Empty boxes share what's left (shown as ~20).
  • Start / Finish – who is on court at the start and at the end of the game. Players you pick as starters move to the top. Pick 5, or just the ones you want; the rest are picked automatically.
  • Pos – tap to tick every position the player can play (e.g. PG and SG). PG, SG, SF, PF and C are single spots, G is either guard spot, F either forward spot. Nothing ticked ('–') plays anywhere.
  • Reset – back to the default view: everyone in, original order, automatic minutes and no Start/Finish picks. Names and positions stay.
  • Team – save your players once, then pick the team every game.
